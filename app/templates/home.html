What this does, and where it stops

It does

  • Read Form 16, Form 26AS, the AIS (PDF or JSON), bank interest certificates and broker capital-gains exports
  • Reconcile TDS against 26AS and income against the AIS, so mismatches surface before filing rather than in a notice
  • Compute both regimes in full — slabs, 87A with marginal relief, surcharge with marginal relief, cess, and interest under 234A/B/C
  • Pick the right ITR form and explain why
  • Generate the ITR JSON and a field-by-field filing pack

It does not

  • Submit the return. Direct API access needs an e-Return Intermediary licence, which needs a registered entity, a ₹1 crore net worth and a CISA due-diligence certificate. You upload the JSON yourself.
  • Handle business income with books of account (ITR-3), or non-resident taxation
  • Replace a chartered accountant on anything genuinely contentious
