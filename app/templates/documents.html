Password-protected files

AIS and 26AS PDFs are encrypted. The password is your PAN in lower case followed by your date of birth as DDMMYYYY — abcde1234f01011990.

Those are tried automatically once your PAN and date of birth are known. If nothing has been read yet, fill them in on the Income page first, then come back and upload.
